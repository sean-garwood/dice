🎲 Ten Thousand

New game

First to land on exactly 10,000 wins. Go over and you bust!

Rules

  • Win by reaching exactly 10,000 points. If your banked score plus points on the table ever exceeds 10,000, you bust: your turn ends and you lose everything accrued this turn.
  • On your turn, roll all five dice. After each roll you must set aside at least one scoring die; every die you set aside must add to the score.
  • Scoring: a lone 5 = 50 · a lone 1 = 100 · three of a kind = 100 × face value (three 2s = 200), except three 1s = 1,000 · a five-dice straight (1–5 or 2–6) = 1,000.
  • You may not break up a three-of-a-kind if doing so would lower the points on the table — keep all three or leave them all behind.
  • After setting dice aside, either bank your table points and end your turn, or reroll the remaining dice to build a bigger total.
  • If a roll contains no scoring dice, that's a farkle: your turn ends and the table points blow up.
  • If all five dice score, that's hot dice — pick all five back up and keep rolling with your points intact.
